## What it does

MovieMind helps users discover movies through content-based recommendation. Search a film title, read its metadata and see the other films the engine considers similar, with the evidence behind every claim shown on screen.

## How the recommendation works

The engine is fixed-configuration retrieval over a sparse TF-IDF matrix of 5,000 films, built once at startup. Similarity is computed at request time using cosine similarity over L2-normalised per-field TF-IDF blocks. Genre weight is 0.44 and an evidence floor of three shared terms keeps results readable. Similarity is reported as a raw score, never a percentage, and returned-versus-examined counts are always visible.

## What users see

A TMDb attribution notice is always on screen, even when the API is unreachable. The frontend renders the backend's ranking order exactly as received — it does not re-sort, re-score, filter or reformat results.
