## What it does

WriteTrace estimates whether a piece of writing shows stylistic patterns associated with machine-generated text. It reports a calibrated machine/human likelihood, a 5-level classification, a confidence signal and an explicit insufficient-evidence state for short texts.

## Why it exists

Generative models now produce fluent prose everywhere. WriteTrace is built to make detector fragility visible: honest evaluation, calibrated uncertainty and documented failure modes are features, not embarrassments. It is a statistical estimate, not proof.

## How the model works

Handcrafted lexical, sentence-level, syntactic and stylometric features feed classical ML models. The selected winner uses 89 features and is trained from the artifact in `ml/models/final-v1/`. Predictions come from the trained model — never from keywords, regex or an external LLM API. Probabilities are calibrated and a reliability gate abstains on short inputs.

## Evaluation

Measured and published in the project docs: baselines, model selection, calibration and a locked test evaluation. The headline result on the MAGE test set is ROC-AUC 0.9618, PR-AUC 0.9799, F1(macro) 0.8856, Brier 0.0739 and ECE-10 0.0058, with out-of-distribution results and per-length slices reported alongside their failure modes.
